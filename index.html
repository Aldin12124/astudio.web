A few answers

Good to know.

Some helpful details before we start building together.

What kinds of websites do you create?

We build business, e-commerce, restaurant, portfolio and landing-page websites, and redesign existing sites.

How is the price decided?

Every quote is tailored to the project’s scope, features and goals. Share a few details and we’ll suggest a suitable package.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the site’s scope, how soon the content is ready and feedback along the way. We’ll agree on a timeline after discussing your brief.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief about your business, goals and audience is a good start. Your logo, photos, text or examples you like are helpful too.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. Website redesigns are one of our services. We can review your current site and discuss what to keep, improve or rebuild.

What happens after I contact you?

We’ll discuss your goals and project scope, then outline a suitable next step and prepare a tailored quote.
